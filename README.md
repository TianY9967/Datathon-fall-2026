QSED — Quick Safety Exploration Dashboard

Turning Historical Workplace Injuries into Practical Safety Insights

QSED is a free, interactive workplace safety dashboard designed for small and newly established businesses that lack their own injury databases or the budget for expensive safety analysis software.

Using publicly available OSHA severe injury reports, QSED helps safety officers explore historical workplace injuries, identify potential hazards, and prepare evidence-informed employee safety training.

Key Features

1. Workplace Injury Exploration

Visualizes historical OSHA severe injury reports across industries, helping users understand injury patterns and trends.

2. Workplace Hazard Scenario Analysis

Allows users to select industry sectors, event types, injury sources, and states to explore historical incident frequencies and model-estimated amputation risks.

3. Incident Similarity Finder

Enables users to search for relevant historical incidents using keywords. Through text similarity analysis, QSED retrieves comparable workplace injury cases.

4. Incident Case Briefs

Provides structured summaries of selected incidents, highlighting reported circumstances, potential hazards, and discussion questions that safety officers can present to trainees.

Why QSED?

New businesses may have limited access to historical workplace injury information and professional safety analytics tools.

QSED helps bridge this gap by making public injury data easier to explore and apply. Instead of starting safety training without relevant examples, safety officers can use real incidents to support hazard awareness and workplace safety discussions.

QSED turns public workplace injury data into accessible insights for safer, more informed training.
